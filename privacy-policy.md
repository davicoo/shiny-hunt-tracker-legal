# Privacy Policy for Shiny Hunt Tracker

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

## Overview

Shiny Hunt Tracker ("the App") is operated by DWC Stuff ("we", "us", or "our").

This policy is short because the App collects nothing. There is no account, no
analytics and no networking code of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your
device, and none of it is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

## Information We Collect

**None.** We receive no information about you or your use of the App.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following in its own private storage on your device. This
data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it yourself.

- **Hunt records**: species, game, hunt method, encounter counts, timers,
  phases, dates, nicknames and notes you enter
- **Photos**: images you pick from your photo library and attach to a hunt,
  copied into the App's storage at a reduced size
- **Settings**: your preferences, such as your default game and whether
  regional forms count separately

## Photos

If you attach a picture to a hunt, the App asks permission to read your photo
library and copies the image you pick into its own storage. It reads only the
images you choose. It does not scan your library, and it does not upload images
anywhere.

## Backups

You can export your history to a `.shinyhunt` file. The App writes that file on
your device and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes from
there is entirely your choice — Files, iCloud Drive, email, or nowhere at all.
The App does not send it anywhere on its own, and we never receive it.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- No account, sign-in, email address or name
- No analytics, usage statistics, crash reporting or telemetry
- No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites
- No location data
- No contacts, calendars or health data
- No third-party SDKs — the App uses only Apple's own frameworks

The App makes no network requests. There is no server behind it, because there
is nothing to send to one.

## Pokédex Data

Species and form names ship inside the App as a bundled file, compiled in
advance from [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co). The App does not contact PokéAPI or
any other service while you use it. The App includes no Pokémon artwork; every
image in your collection is one you added yourself.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept on your device for as long as you keep the App. Deleting the
App removes everything it stored, including the photos held within it. There is
no copy anywhere else for you to request or for us to erase. If you want to keep
your history first, export a backup before deleting.

## Children's Privacy

The App gathers no personal information from anyone, children included.

## Your Rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct
or delete. Your records are yours and sit on your device, where you can view and
edit them in the App, export them at any time, or remove them by deleting the
App.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant
changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Any change to what the App
collects would also require an app update that you would have to install.
